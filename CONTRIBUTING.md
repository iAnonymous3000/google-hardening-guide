# Contributing

This guide is only useful if it is accurate today. Google moves settings, renames features and retires products, so corrections are the most valuable contribution you can make.

> [!CAUTION]
> Never post your email address, phone number, verification codes or screenshots with personal details in an issue. The maintainers cannot recover Google accounts. If you are locked out, use [g.co/recover](https://g.co/recover) and ignore anyone who offers to do it for you.

## Report something outdated

[Open an issue](https://github.com/iAnonymous3000/google-hardening-guide/issues/new/choose) and pick **Outdated or wrong**. Include the section, what you see now, and a source. Cropped screenshots help.

## Standards for changes

1. **Source every claim.** Prefer Google's own help center, blogs and release notes. Use reputable security research or press only to fill gaps, and label it.
2. **Link the exact setting.** Use the deepest stable link on myaccount.google.com, myactivity.google.com or Gmail settings, and test it while signed in.
3. **Tag the level.** Baseline is for everyone and costs nothing. Enhanced takes more effort or a purchase. Maximum trades convenience for safety. The README and issue forms use these definitions.
4. **Explain why in one line.** People follow advice they understand.
5. **Write plainly.** Short sentences, active voice, and no jargon without a definition. No em or en dashes: use a period, a comma or a new sentence. CI enforces this.
6. **Date loosely.** Use month and year, or "since mid-2026", rather than exact dates.
7. **Stay vendor neutral.** Recommend standards such as FIDO2 and passkeys before brands. No affiliate links.

## Visuals

The banner and the sign-in strength diagram are light and dark SVGs in `assets/`, generated by [`scripts/build_visuals.py`](scripts/build_visuals.py). Edit the script, never the SVGs, then run:

```sh
python3 scripts/build_visuals.py --check
```

It rewrites the SVGs and checks that every label fits in a wide fallback font. It needs Pillow (`pip install pillow`) and DejaVu Sans or Arial. Commit what changes.

It also writes `assets/social-preview.svg`. Export that to `assets/social-preview.png` at 1280 x 640 and set it under the repository's **Settings** > **Social preview**.

## Review cycle

When you verify the whole guide against current Google documentation, update the **Last reviewed** badge in the README, and add an entry to [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## Automated checks

Every pull request and every push to main checks:

- no em or en dashes
- `--check` passes and `assets/` matches what the script writes
- links in the Markdown files, including `#anchors`. Gmail settings, Gemini, X, `chrome://` and new-issue links are skipped, so test them by hand.

The link check also runs monthly and opens an issue when links break.
