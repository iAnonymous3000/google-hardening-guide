# Changelog

Notable changes to the guide. Dates are month and year.

## October 2026

Restructured into Ground rules, three levels and hacked-account steps, then checked against the README's sources.

- The printable checklist is merged into the README levels.
- The attack-surface and levels diagrams, the flowchart and the Quick links table are removed. The sign-in chart shows tiers, not scores.
- Corrected:
  - Create passkeys only on devices you own and do not share.
  - The carrier lock and Scam Detection are Baseline.
  - The AT&T lock is set only in the AT&T app.
  - Storage and payment warnings can be real, so check them yourself.
  - Advanced Protection does not allow backup-code downloads, recovery contacts, selfie video or scheduled Takeout.
  - A password change revokes app passwords.
  - Google promises to disconnect reviewed Gemini chats from your account only when it sends them to outside providers.
- Advice that no longer holds is in [Outdated advice to ignore](README.md#outdated-advice-to-ignore).

- First version: passwords, two-factor authentication, phishing, recovery, privacy settings and Advanced Protection.
