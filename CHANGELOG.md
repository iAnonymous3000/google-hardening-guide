# Changelog

Notable changes to the guide. Dates are month and year.

## October 2026

A complete rewrite, reviewed against Google's own documentation as of October 2026.

### Added

- A 20-minute baseline with direct settings links, and three levels: Baseline, Enhanced and Maximum
- A threat model: real-time phishing kits, infostealer cookie theft, SIM swaps, consent phishing, fake Google support calls, weak recovery and password reuse
- Passkeys, **Skip password when possible**, and how passkeys interact with 2-Step Verification
- Recovery contacts, selfie video verification, and the 7-day trust period for new sign-in methods
- Linked apps (formerly third-party access), including AI agent access
- A Gmail audit table, the IMAP and **Send mail as** changes, and the Gmailify and POP retirement
- Calendar invite filtering, Drive blocking, Photos, YouTube and payment settings
- Chrome: Enhanced Safe Browsing, Device Bound Session Credentials, HTTPS by default and third-party cookies
- Android theft protection, Identity Check, Find Hub and Advanced Protection device mode, including the Android 17 additions
- iPhone: passkeys, Stolen Device Protection and Lockdown Mode
- Carrier SIM and port-out locks for T-Mobile, Verizon and AT&T
- The Advanced Protection Program with passkey enrollment
- Privacy controls: Search Services History, on-device Timeline, My Ad Center, Gemini Keep Activity and connected apps, Results about you
- A compromised-account runbook with a flowchart
- A maintenance schedule, Workspace notes, an outdated-advice table, a quick links table and grouped sources
- Light and dark diagrams, a printable [CHECKLIST.md](CHECKLIST.md), a contributing guide, issue forms, and automated link and style checks

### Removed or corrected

- Forced 90-day password changes, which contradict NIST SP 800-63B-4
- Google's dark web report, discontinued in early 2026
- The Password Alert extension, whose source Google archived in 2026
- Customizable security alerts, which personal accounts do not have
- Contacting Google Support to recover an account, which Google does not offer
- The claim that Advanced Protection requires two physical keys; one passkey is enough since 2024
- Reviewing Location History at google.com/maps/timeline; Timeline moved to your device
- adssettings.google.com as the main ad control, now My Ad Center
- Gmail confidential mode as a privacy tool; it is not end-to-end encrypted
- A VPN on public Wi-Fi as an account security control

## 2024

- First version: passwords, two-factor authentication, phishing, recovery, privacy settings and Advanced Protection.
